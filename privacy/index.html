Privacy policy

Version 0.2 (draft), 9 October 2026. Applies to the Swotty app and this website.

Swotty is a study app made by Egghead Labs, a South African software company. This policy explains what personal information Swotty handles, why, who receives it, how long we keep it and what you can do about it. Swotty is offline-first: you can study without an account and without AI, and in that case nothing about your study is sent to us.

Who is responsible

Egghead Labs is the responsible party under the Protection of Personal Information Act, 2013 (POPIA) and the controller under the EU General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) where it applies. Contact: support@eggheadlabs.co.za. Our registered name, registration number, postal address and the name of our Information Officer will be added here before this policy takes effect.

Information we collect

On your device only. Your imported papers, memos, notes, photos, answers, links and progress stay on your device for local study. Scanned pages are read on your device and you check the result. Manual study works with AI off. Swotty does not distribute your uploads or supply its own exam library.

If you create an account. Accounts are optional. You sign in with your email address and a one-time code; there is no password. When you ask for a code, your email goes to our account service and our email relay. Your account record holds your user identifier, sessions, declared birth year, a conservative age band, declared country (not taken from GPS), the store's age range where available, and timestamped records of the consent version you accepted and of any guardian consent. If guardian consent is needed, we use a separate guardian email address. We do not ask for an identity document, your address book or your full date of birth.

If you turn on sync. With your consent, private sync stores document hashes, types, page counts, chunk positions and geometry, links, attempts, mastery and review cards. This structural snapshot leaves out the raw text of your documents and your answers. Free users re-upload source files on a new device.

If you have paid access and use cloud backup. Cloud backup of source documents and captured page images is being introduced in phases for eligible paid users, so you can restore them on another device. These files are stored in your private cloud space and can contain personal information.

If you buy something. Purchases go through Google Play billing (and the Apple App Store if an iOS version is released). Google or Apple handles your payment details; we never receive your card number. We receive purchase product identifiers, verification tokens or signed transactions, and we keep entitlement and usage records. We share an obfuscated account identifier with the store's billing service to keep purchases secure.

If you use AI features. See the AI section below.

If you choose to share study signals. Optional structural signals help improve shared document structure. They exclude document text, filenames and precise location. They are off by default and controlled in Settings. Learners treated as minors (see Younger learners) do not contribute corrections, and further restrictions apply to them.

When you contact us. Your email address and what you write to us.

Automatically. Our servers and hosting providers handle IP addresses and request logs to run and protect the service. Swotty contains no third-party analytics SDK, no advertising SDK and no first-party crash uploader.

How we use your information

  • To provide the features you choose: sign-in, sync, restore, AI requests and purchase checks.
  • To keep the service secure and prevent fraud, including purchase abuse.
  • To answer support requests and handle your privacy requests.
  • To improve shared document structure from optional signals, using only anonymous totals with at least 20 contributors.
  • To meet legal obligations.

We do not sell your information, and we do not use your study content for advertising.

AI features and the companies that process AI requests

AI is optional and is not a marking authority. When you ask for AI help, we send only the study material needed for that task: the question, memo or reference excerpts and, when you compare an answer, the answer you submitted. Our service keeps your learner identifier for budgets, queues, caching and accounting, but does not include it as an identifier in what we send to the AI provider. We never send your account email, birth year, guardian records or payment details to an AI provider. Study text itself can contain personal information, so remove names and other identifiers you do not need before asking for AI; the answer-comparison path also removes identifiers automatically.

AI requests are processed for us by these companies, chosen per task and with fallbacks:

  • OpenAI (United States)
  • Mistral AI (France), including Mistral's document reading service when paid server reading of a scanned page is used
  • Alibaba Cloud Model Studio (international service)

They process the material and return a result. We may keep the results in our private queues, cache and usage ledger. For a public exam paper, results that depend only on the paper (such as explanations and drill sets) are computed once and shared across learners; they are stored without any learner's identity. Online material is only included if you opt in. In the Google Play Data safety form we declare this as data shared with these processors.

Who we share information with

  • AI processors listed above, for AI requests you make.
  • Hosting and storage: Hetzner (Germany) hosts our service; Backblaze B2 (EU region) stores document backups; Hetzner Object Storage holds database backups and erasure records.
  • Email relay that sends sign-in codes and guardian emails (provider and country to be confirmed before launch).
  • Google (and Apple, if released there) for store billing and age signals.
  • Professional advisers and authorities where the law requires it, and a buyer or successor of our business under the same protections.

Each provider may only use your information to provide its service to us.

International transfers

Swotty is run from South Africa, and our service is hosted in the European Union, so account, sync and backup data leave South Africa. AI requests may be processed in the United States, the European Union or elsewhere, depending on the provider. Under section 72 of POPIA we only transfer personal information where the recipient is bound by law, binding corporate rules or an agreement giving protection substantially similar to POPIA, or with your consent, or where another ground in section 72 applies. For people in the EU, transfers outside the EEA rely on adequacy decisions or standard contractual clauses.

How long we keep information

  • Ordinary private cloud records: deleted after 90 days of account inactivity.
  • Paid private data, source files and reusable AI answers: kept while you have paid access and for 90 days after it ends.
  • Optional raw signals: at most 90 days, and deleted once folded into anonymous totals.
  • After deletion, a minimal erasure and fraud record may remain so deleted data or purchases are not restored by mistake.
  • Anonymous totals that no longer identify anyone may remain.

Your choices and rights

Delete your account. In the app, go to Account and choose Delete account. This removes account-owned private data, ends your sessions, cancels queued AI requests and removes raw signals and account ledger data. You can also request deletion without the app. Files on your device stay until you remove them. Deleting your account does not cancel a subscription; cancel it in Google Play (or the App Store).

Download your data. In Account, choose to download your account data.

Your POPIA rights. You may ask whether we hold personal information about you, ask for a copy, ask us to correct or delete it, object to processing based on our legitimate interests, and withdraw consent at any time (this does not affect processing before you withdrew). Use the in-app controls or email support@eggheadlabs.co.za. We check that you control the account; we do not ask for identity documents or passwords.

Complaints. If you are not satisfied with our answer, you may complain to the Information Regulator (South Africa) at inforegulator.org.za.

Younger learners

Swotty is aimed at learners aged 16 and older. That is who we market to; it is not the minimum age for every feature.

  • No one under 13 may open an account. Offline study without an account is available from age 13, with nothing sent to us.
  • Learners from 13 up to the adult age where they live are treated as minors, with extra protections.
  • Under POPIA, a person under 18 is a child, and their personal information may generally be processed only with the consent of a competent person, such as a parent or guardian. For learners under 18 in South Africa, account sync and paid AI therefore need a parent-approved store signal or our guardian consent route.
  • In other countries, account features below the local digital consent age need the same kind of consent; where no consent route exists yet, offline study remains available.
  • Age is self-declared, checked against store age signals where available, and we apply the more cautious age band.

Security

Access controls and encrypted connections are built into the service. Before launch we will verify production encryption, logging, backup erasure and our breach procedure. If a security compromise affects your personal information, we will tell you and the Information Regulator as POPIA requires.

People in the European Union

If you are in the EU or EEA, the GDPR may apply.

  • Legal bases: providing the features you sign up for is necessary to perform our contract with you (Article 6(1)(b)); sync, optional signals and online material rely on your consent (Article 6(1)(a)); security, fraud prevention and purchase checks rely on our legitimate interests (Article 6(1)(f)); we keep some records because the law requires it (Article 6(1)(c)).
  • Children: where a learner is below the digital consent age of their country (13 to 16 under Article 8), account features need consent from a holder of parental responsibility.
  • Your rights: access, correction, deletion, restriction, portability, objection, and withdrawal of consent.
  • Complaints: you may complain to the data protection authority where you live or work.

Changes to this policy

We will date every change here. If a change is material, we will tell you in the app and ask for your consent again where the law requires it.

What you must check with counsel

  1. Under-18 learners: whether Swotty's rule (accounts from 13, minor protections to the local adult age, competent-person consent for sync and paid AI) meets POPIA sections 34 and 35, and whether a store age signal counts as consent of a competent person.
  2. Section 72 transfer basis for each processor: Hetzner and Backblaze (EU), OpenAI (US), Mistral (France) and Alibaba Cloud Model Studio international (processing country to confirm), and whether consent is needed for any of them.
  3. Processor contracts: confirm each AI provider's contracting entity, data processing terms, no-training commitment and retention period for the routes enabled at launch.
  4. Lawful basis and retention period for the minimal erasure and fraud record kept after deletion, and backup expiry.
  5. Whether Swotty must register with, or notify, the Information Regulator before processing children's information, and the Information Officer's registration.
  6. Name and country of the email relay, and whether GDPR Article 27 requires an EU representative.
