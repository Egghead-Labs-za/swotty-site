How it works

  1. 1

    Upload

    Add past papers, memos and notes. A photo of a page works too.

  2. 2

    Answer and mark

    Swotty splits each paper into questions. Answer one, then open the memo and mark yourself.

  3. 3

    Practise what is shaky

    Each topic shows strong, shaky or learn. Flashcards and reviews bring shaky topics back. A study timer keeps sessions short.

Free with AI off. AI is optional. Turn it on to get help with a question; each use counts against an allowance.

What you keep private

  • Your files stay on your device. Papers, notes and answers live on your phone unless you choose sync.
  • AI sees the question, not you. We never send your email, birth year or payment details to an AI provider.
  • No ads, no trackers. No advertising or third-party analytics. We do not sell your study content.
