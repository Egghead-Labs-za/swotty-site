2. Who can use Swotty

Swotty is aimed at learners aged 16 and older. No one under 13 may open an account. Learners from 13 up to the adult age where they live are treated as minors: some features (account sync and paid AI) need the consent of a parent or guardian, given through the store's parent-approved signal or our guardian consent route. Under South African law (POPIA), a person under 18 is a child, and their personal information may generally be processed only with the consent of a competent person. If you are a parent or guardian giving consent, you agree to these terms on the learner's behalf.
